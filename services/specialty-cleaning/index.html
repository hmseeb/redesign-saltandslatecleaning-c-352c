Premium homes feature premium materials, and those materials demand more than a spray bottle and a microfiber cloth. Natural stone countertops need pH-balanced cleaners that will not etch the surface. Hardwood floors require moisture-controlled cleaning that will not warp or dull the finish. Radiant heated floors need products that will not leave residue that interferes with heat transfer. These are not details you can afford to get wrong.

Salt & Slate specialty cleaning is a focused service for homeowners who need expert-level care for specific surfaces and features. Whether it is a marble foyer, a wine cellar, a home gym with rubber flooring, a steam shower with natural stone tile, or a chef kitchen with custom stone and integrated appliances, we bring the knowledge and products that each surface requires. Our teams are trained to identify surface materials on sight and select the appropriate cleaning approach without guessing.

This service can be booked as a standalone visit for homes that need targeted attention on specific areas, or added to any recurring or deep cleaning package. Many clients use specialty cleaning on a quarterly rotation, addressing different high-maintenance surfaces each visit to keep everything in peak condition without the expense of a full specialty clean every time.

What’s Included

Every specialty cleaning follows a detailed checklist so nothing gets missed. Here is what your team covers on every visit.

The Process

How it works

Surface inventory

We identify every specialty surface in your home, note the material and finish, and create a care plan. If you have manufacturer care instructions, we incorporate those.

Product matching

Each surface gets paired with the correct cleaning product and technique. We bring surface-specific solutions, not all-purpose cleaners that risk damage.

Targeted cleaning

Our team works surface by surface, following the care plan. Natural stone is cleaned differently from engineered quartz, and old-growth hardwood is treated differently from engineered planks.

Care guidance

We share maintenance tips and product recommendations so you can protect your surfaces between professional visits. No upselling, just honest guidance on what your home needs.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you know which products to use on my surfaces?

We identify surface materials on the initial consultation — marble versus quartzite, solid hardwood versus engineered, natural stone versus porcelain tile. We bring surface-specific products, not all-purpose cleaners, and we check finish type before selecting application method. If you have manufacturer care documents, share them with us and we incorporate those directly into your surface care plan.

Can I add specialty cleaning to a deep clean or recurring visit?

Yes. Many clients have one or two specialty surfaces — a marble foyer, a wine cellar, a steam shower — that need expert-level attention while the rest of the home gets a standard or deep clean. We can split the visit accordingly. Just flag the surfaces when you book and we'll bring the right products and adjust the time estimate.

What surfaces does specialty cleaning cover?

Natural stone — marble, travertine, granite, slate. Hardwood floors with finish-sensitive care. Wine cellar surfaces and climate condition. Home gym equipment and rubber flooring. Radiant heated floors with residue-free products. Steam showers and wet rooms. Custom range hoods and integrated appliances. If you have a surface you're unsure about, describe it when you book and we'll confirm whether it falls under specialty scope.
