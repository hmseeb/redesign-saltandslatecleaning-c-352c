Move-out cleaning prepares a home for a landlord inspection, a property handover, or the first showing. The home should be primarily empty — large furniture and personal property moved out. Cabinets, drawers, the refrigerator, freezer, and pantry should be cleared so our team can clean inside. Stray items are fine. We don't move or dispose of items left in the home.

We clean inside the refrigerator, oven, cabinets, drawers, and closets. We scrub shower grout, wet-wipe blinds, clean interior windows and tracks, address wall scuffs, detail baseboards, and clean ceilings. Each clean ends with a final walkthrough against landlord and realtor inspection standards.

Electricity and running water must be on at the time of service.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a move-out cleaning help me get my security deposit back?

It's not a guarantee, but it removes cleaning as a line item on the inspection report. Most deposit deductions for cleaning fall apart when you can show the landlord a professional receipt and the unit is clearly in clean condition. A move-out cleaning typically costs less than a standard cleaning deduction.

How is a move-out cleaning different from a deep clean?

Same scope as a move-in cleaning — inside every appliance, inside every cabinet, drawer, and closet, plus a final walkthrough against landlord and realtor inspection criteria. The home should be primarily empty: large furniture and personal property moved out, with cabinets, drawers, the refrigerator, freezer, and pantry cleared so our team can clean inside. Stray items are fine. Pricing and checklist match move-in. The intent is different: move-out is about meeting an external standard and protecting your interests.

What if the home isn't fully empty when you arrive?

The home should be primarily empty — large furniture and personal property moved out. Stray items are fine. Cabinets, drawers, the refrigerator, freezer, and pantry should be cleared so we can clean inside.

Do you move or remove items left in the home?

No. We don't move, store, donate, or discard items left in the home. Cabinets, drawers, the refrigerator, freezer, and pantry should be cleared by you so our team can clean inside them.

Do utilities need to be on for a move-out clean?

Yes. Electricity and running water must be on at the time of service.

Can you work directly with my property manager?

Yes. We can coordinate access and scheduling with a property manager or landlord and provide a completion summary they can keep on file. If there's a specific inspection checklist they use, share it with us and we'll clean to those criteria.
