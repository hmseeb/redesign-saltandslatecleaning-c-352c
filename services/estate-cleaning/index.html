White-glove care for exceptional homes

White-glove cleaning for luxury residences over 5,000 sq ft. Specialty surface care, antique handling, and discretion for high-value properties. Salt & Slate provides estate cleaning, backed by 263 Google reviews across 6 locations.

Get a Free QuoteCall (385) 333-7737

Homes over 5,000 square feet present challenges that standard cleaning services are not equipped to handle. Twelve-foot ceilings require specialized equipment. Natural stone floors demand pH-neutral products applied with precision. Custom millwork, antiques, and art collections need cleaning professionals who understand the difference between lacquer and polyurethane, between marble and quartzite, between a patina that adds value and grime that diminishes it.

Salt & Slate estate cleaning is a white-glove service designed for luxury residences, historic properties, and high-value homes where the wrong product or technique can cause thousands of dollars in damage. Our estate teams are trained in specialty surface care, including natural stone, hardwood, crystal, brass, silver, and fine upholstery. We carry surface-specific products and equipment, and we document the materials in your home during our initial walkthrough so every subsequent visit uses the correct approach.

Discretion is a core component of estate service. We brief every cleaning team on privacy expectations and conduct, coordinate with household staff and property managers, work around your schedule without disruption, and maintain strict professionalism at every visit. Your home is your private space, and we treat it accordingly.

The Process

How it works

Property consultation

We tour your home to document every surface material, identify specialty care requirements, and understand your expectations. This creates the cleaning blueprint for your property.

Team assignment

We assign estate-certified professionals who are trained in the specific surfaces and materials in your home. Your team stays consistent across every visit.

White-glove execution

Each cleaning follows the surface-specific blueprint. Natural stone gets pH-neutral care, hardwood gets appropriate moisture levels, and delicate surfaces get hand cleaning.

Post-visit documentation

After each visit, your team lead provides a summary noting any surface concerns, maintenance recommendations, or items that may need professional restoration.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in an estate clean that isn't in a standard deep clean?

The core checklist is the same as a deep clean, but the execution is different. Estate cleaning uses surface-specific products and equipment — pH-neutral cleaners on natural stone, appropriate moisture levels on hardwood, brass-safe polishes on hardware. We also handle chandelier and high-fixture dusting with professional-grade equipment, coordinate with household staff if applicable, and provide a post-visit surface report that flags anything needing professional restoration.

How do you handle confidentiality?

We sign a confidentiality agreement before the first visit. Our teams are briefed on privacy expectations — no photos, no conversations about the property or its contents outside of the team, and coordination only through the contact you designate.

Who has access to my estate?

Every Salt & Slate cleaning professional is licensed, background-checked, and fully insured. We brief every team on privacy expectations and conduct before the first visit, and the same detailed checklist and 24-hour satisfaction guarantee apply on every cleaning.
