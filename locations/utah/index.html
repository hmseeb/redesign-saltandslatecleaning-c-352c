Frequently Asked Questions

What areas in Utah do you serve?

We serve communities across Summit, Wasatch, Salt Lake, Utah, and Davis counties — from Park City to Payson, Alpine to West Valley City. Check the city list below or contact us if you don't see your city listed.

Do you offer both residential and commercial cleaning in Utah?

Yes. We provide home cleaning services (recurring, deep cleaning, move-in/out, vacation home, Airbnb, estate, and specialty cleaning) and commercial cleaning for 14 industry verticals including medical offices, restaurants, gyms, and property management.

How does Utah's climate affect cleaning needs?

Utah's hard water leaves mineral deposits on glass and fixtures. Winter road salt and inversion-season particulate track indoors. Summer brings construction dust and dry-climate grit. Our teams use products and techniques calibrated to these conditions.
