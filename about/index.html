Who We Are

Not just clean. Cared for.

Salt & Slate serves homeowners and businesses who value quality over convenience. Our clients live in Park City ski homes, Scottsdale desert estates, Salt Lake City Victorians, Las Vegas luxury communities, and Dallas–Fort Worth estates. They expect attention to detail, consistency, and professionals they can trust with a key to their home.

We match that expectation by sending licensed, background-checked, and fully insured cleaning professionals to every job, working from a detailed room-by-room checklist customized to your home — which surfaces need special care, which rooms you prioritize, and the preferences that make a clean feel like yours.

Every cleaning is backed by our 24-hour satisfaction guarantee. If anything falls short, we return to make it right at no additional charge.
